## The direction
- **Each pattern gets an arc.** In order:
  - A: what it is, in one sentence;
  - B: why it matters;
  - C: how big it is, as one number plus one visual;
  - D: one or two examples told well, linking to the loved dossiers;
  - E: what to look at next.
- **The minimum viable version** is one infographic per pattern («honestly just give me an infographic on each one»), with one clear finding each.
- **The process** is a crafted round: several agents propose and an editor ranks, research comes before writing ([[feedback-research-before-writing]]), story before data ([[feedback-writing-story-craft]]), and there is an independent review. The owner sees renders before anything is built.
